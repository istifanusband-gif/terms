Refund Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025

1. Free Trial

NovaDesk offers a 14-day free trial for all new users. You will not be charged during the trial period. You may cancel at any time before the trial ends without any obligation.

2. Subscription Refunds

If you are not satisfied with NovaDesk within the first 7 days of your paid subscription, you may request a full refund. After 7 days, refunds are not provided for the current billing period, but you may cancel to prevent future charges.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at hello@novadesk.io with your account email and reason for the refund. We will process eligible refunds within 5-10 business days to your original payment method.

4. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You will continue to have access to the Service until then.

5. Exceptions

Refunds will not be issued for accounts that have violated our Terms of Service, or for partial months of service after the 7-day refund window.

6. Contact

For refund questions, contact us at hello@novadesk.io. We aim to respond within 24 hours.
